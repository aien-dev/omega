# R9 generation barrier

Status: host proof. The organism keeps one active generation while a candidate
is prepared beside it. Promotion is the only moment the barrier is strict,
and it is strict for the candidate. Live reactions are not stopped.

After a stop at any persistence step, recovery selects the previous
generation or the next generation as a whole. It does not splice them.

The candidate may describe the next generation. The promotion right on the
native authority is what permits the transition, and that right cannot be
handed on. A candidate that presents itself as the authority is refused.
The Linux checker remains and must report the same authority results.

This proof does not start the graphics processor, connect the real model,
connect the real Omega, or replace the resident body.
