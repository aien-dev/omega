## 1. Executive Summary & Doctrine

Milestone 10 established the verified program library (`OMEGA_LIBRARY_V1`), enabling storage and retrieval of verified components.
Milestone 11 establishes **autonomous abstraction discovery** (`OMEGA_LIBRARY_DISCOVERY`):

> **THE MACHINE MUST INVENT ITS OWN ABSTRACTIONS. IT EXTRACTS COMMON MEANINGS FROM PAST SOLUTIONS, PROVES THEM VALID, AND ADMITS THEM TO COMPRESS REASONING.**

### The Four M11 Invariants:
1. **Corpus Compression**: Discovered abstraction compresses multiple verified programs across the corpus, reducing total description length ($\Delta \text{Cost} > 0$).
2. **Semantic Preservation**: Factoring the abstraction into corpus programs preserves exact input-output behavior on all validation domains ($f_{factored}(x) == f_{original}(x)$).
3. **Reused on Held-Out Tasks**: The discovered abstraction is successfully reused to solve previously unseen held-out synthesis tasks.
4. **Search Cost Reduction**: The discovered abstraction reduces synthesis search depth and candidate exploration count compared to the primitive baseline.

---

## 2. Abstraction Discovery Architecture

### 2.1 Corpus Representation (`OmegaCorpus`)
A corpus contains verified programs $P_1, \dots, P_N$ generated by synthesis or manual specification to solve a collection of computational tasks.

### 2.2 Common Sub-Expression Mining
The discovery engine enumerates contiguous instruction sub-sequences and composition slices across the corpus programs.
Candidates occurring with frequency $\ge 2$ across distinct programs are extracted as candidate abstractions.

### 2.3 Non-Triviality & Utility Scoring
- **Non-triviality**: Must contain at least two operations (cannot be an existing primitive).
- **Utility / Compression Metric**:
  $$\Delta \text{Cost}(A) = \sum_{P \in \text{Occurrences}(A)} (\text{Cost}(P) - \text{Cost}(P/A)) - \text{Cost}(A)$$
  where $\text{Cost}(P/A)$ is the cost of program $P$ refactored to call abstraction $A$ as a single component.

### 2.4 Verification Ladder & Admission
Every proposed abstraction is subjected to the full M7 verification engine:
- **$V_0$ Structural/Type**: Alignment, bounds, valid DAG, terminal `ret`.
- **$V_1$ Differential**: Exact parity against reference semantics across test domains.
- **$V_2$ Property**: Overflow wrapping, monotonic bounds.
Only abstractions passing all three stages are admitted into `OmegaLibrary`.

## 3. Qualification Gates

1. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_CORPUS_MINING_PASS`: Extract recurring sub-programs from corpus.
2. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_NONTRIVIAL_PASS`: Reject trivial or single-op candidates.
3. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_COMPRESSION_PASS`: Verify positive description length reduction ($\Delta \text{Cost} > 0$).
4. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_SEMANTIC_PRESERVATION_PASS`: Behavioral equivalence preserved after refactoring.
5. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_V0_STRUCTURAL_PASS`: M7 V0 verification passes on discovered abstraction.
6. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_V1_DIFFERENTIAL_PASS`: M7 V1 differential verification passes on discovered abstraction.
7. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_V2_PROPERTY_PASS`: M7 V2 property verification passes on discovered abstraction.
8. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_LIBRARY_ADMISSION_PASS`: Discovered abstraction admitted to library catalog.
9. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_SEARCH_ACCELERATION_PASS`: Held-out task solved at lower depth / candidate count.
10. `OMEGA_DISCOVERY_RECEIPT_PASS`: Master qualification receipt generated with full provenance.
